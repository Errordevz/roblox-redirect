Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-17

demo-mcp is a private Cloudflare Worker that lets you link your own Roblox account via Roblox OAuth 2.0 (PKCE S256) to show your profile/inventory in DEMO.

Data we collect

  • Roblox user ID (sub), username, display name, profile URL, headshot, and granted scopes (openid profile only).
  • OAuth tokens (access/refresh) - encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM in a Durable Object, never in browser storage, never logged, never shared.

What we don't

No password, no .ROBLOSECURITY cookie, no friends/messages/Robux, no writes, no scraping, no selling, no analytics.

How we use

Only to call apis.roblox.com/oauth/v1/userinfo and Open Cloud for your own account. Tokens auto-expire (15m/90d) and are revoked on logout.
